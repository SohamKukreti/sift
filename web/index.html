sift

Give it a website and a question. It goes through the pages one by one, throws out the junk, and keeps the page that has your answer.

Your keys go only to Crawl4AI and OpenRouter. No key yet? Get one at api.crawl4ai.com and openrouter.ai.

knobs

Separate several words with commas. Link words are matched against the URL, so pick URL-like words: schedule, faq, fees.

the pile

Each page gets a score from Jev: how sure it is that the page answers you. Past the line means we read it.

    sift is open source. the code, and a command line version if you like terminals better. Pages are fetched by crawl4ai.
